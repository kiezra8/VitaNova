# VitaNova — Uganda Clinical Guidelines (UCG) 2023

VitaNova is an offline-first Progressive Web Application (PWA) designed for healthcare providers in Uganda. It offers comprehensive clinical guidelines, differential symptom checking, and a dedicated dispensary drug reference based on the **Uganda Clinical Guidelines 2023** (Ministry of Health).

## Features

- **Clinical Disease Guide**: Detailed diagnostic criteria, management plans, and referral protocols for all conditions.
- **Symptom Checker & Differential Diagnosis**: Enter presenting signs and symptoms to receive ranked differential diagnoses.
- **Dispensary & Treatment Lines**: Look up any disease to see specific drug regimens categorized by:
  - First-line & Second-line therapies
  - Prophylaxis & Maintenance
  - Paediatric & Pregnancy-safe dosing
  - Severe / Emergency IV protocols
- **Women & Children's Health Priority**: Dedicated sections for ANC, obstetric emergencies, IMCI, neonatology, and paediatric dosing.
- **100% Offline Capability**: Built with Service Worker and IndexedDB for instant, reliable access in areas with low or no connectivity.

## Deployment

Deployable instantly on **Cloudflare Pages** or **GitHub Pages** as a static PWA without build steps.
